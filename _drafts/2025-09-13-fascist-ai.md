## Divine Electricity

Most of us have heard about Nazi occultism. Robert Evans dives into the origins of this strange brand of racist mysticism, and one of the main figures is [Jörg Lanz von Liebenfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B6rg_Lanz_von_Liebenfels). This Austrian, born Adolf Josef Lanz (but that didn't evoke the sense of nobility of a "von", so he changed it), was a founder of Ariosophy. The "wisdom of the Aryans" was an ideological system that harkened back to a falsely-reified singular ancient Germanic culture and created a stark divide between the "higher" and "lower" races.

Shit gets weird (well, weird*er*) when he starts talking about [interstellar deities who bred by electricity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariosophy#Theozoology) giving birth to the Aryan people in a book titled *Theozoology, or the Science of the Sodomite-Apelings and the Divine Electron*.

> Liebenfels convinces himself that like Christ must have been like this being of electricity, cuz that's the sexy new technology. And our modern fascists have convinced themselves that using AI, they're going to make a God...rule the future. It's the fact that all these Silicon Valley founders have gotten in bed with fascism isn't a deviation from the norm. It's not weird.
  
  
>And a lot of this is rooted in this idea of if you're obsessed with modernity and these new ideas and new technologies, part of what you have to do is sweep away the old and democracy is the idea that people deserve rights is old. We don't have time for that. Right. The future, like, you know, whatever it is we're doing with this new technology is more important than these archaic ideas of human rights.
